ABOUT US

Why we started

We're students at a STEM school where research is a huge part of our education — but being surrounded by research doesn't mean anyone teaches you how to actually do it.

The gap we noticed

Students often know how to search for papers, use Google Scholar, or build a project — but still struggle to read research effectively, understand existing work, identify gaps, form real research questions, and build a methodology.

What we're changing

Research Compass exists so students don't have to figure it all out alone. We want every member to move from "I don't know where to start" to "I understand the process and can develop my own research direction."

Our Vision

A community where students explore research independently, collaborate, learn from experienced people, and eventually contribute their own ideas — growing beyond one school into a broader student research network.

Our Mission

  • Make research more accessible to students
  • Give students a structured pathway for learning research
  • Build research skills, not just hand out opportunities
  • Create a community for learning and support
  • Connect students with mentors, researchers, and organizations
  • Encourage original research questions and ideas
